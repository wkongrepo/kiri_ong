The adventure

Trade Route Quest is a pick-your-path series for ages 8 to 12. In the first book, your child joins a Sogdian merchant caravan from Tang Chang’an to Samarkand, deciding what to sell, who to trust and how to keep calm on the way.

About the series and author

Kiri Ong is an educator who writes historical adventures that teach children to handle money wisely, steady their feelings and respect people from other cultures.
