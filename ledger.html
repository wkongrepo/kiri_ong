Free printable ledger

Print this for any Trade Route Quest adventure. Two pages: a ledger for your Coins and Goods, and a page for nerves, skills and the “When your Coins go below zero” guide.

Download the ledger (PDF)

How to use it

  1. Print both pages (A5, or scale to fit A4 or Letter).
  2. Write your starting totals on the first line of each column, then each new total on the next empty line. The last number is what you have now.
  3. Put a coin or paperclip on your nerves track, and write in and tick the skills your book tells you to pick.
  4. If your coins go below zero, use the guide on the second page.

This ledger works for every book in the series, so it has no story details on it.

Kiri Ong is an educator who writes historical adventures that teach children to handle money wisely, steady their feelings and respect people from other cultures.
