6 Money Lessons From a Silk Road Caravan

Merchants on the road faced scams, costs and tough choices. Their lessons still work today.

  1. The same thing costs different amounts in different places

    Silk was cheap where it was made and dearer the further west it went. A trader who knows this can carry goods to where they are worth more.

    Try it: Look at one item in two shops. Why might the prices differ?

  2. If a deal is too good and too fast, ask why

    In Trade Route Quest, a stranger in a red cap offers double the price, right now. Real buyers can wait while you check. Pressure to hurry is a warning sign.

    Try it: Practise saying: “I would like to check first.”

  3. Count twice, smile once

    Check the weights, the coins and the change, even when the other person is friendly. Kind people will not mind. Careless counting costs the most.

    Try it: Let your child count your change at the next shop.

  4. Waiting and rushing both cost money

    On the road, every extra day costs food and wages. But racing into danger can cost far more. Wise travellers ask what each choice really costs.

    Try it: Pick a choice this week and list its costs in money, time and worry.

  5. Keep some coins in reserve

    A merchant who spends every coin has nothing left when the road gets hard. Keeping a little back is what lets you keep going.

    Try it: Set aside one coin from pocket money for “surprises”.

  6. Borrowing has a price

    In the book, Bars lends you coins on the road home, but wants a little extra for the favour. A debt is a promise, and it can grow.

    Try it: Ask: if I borrow 5 and must pay back 6, what did the loan cost me?
