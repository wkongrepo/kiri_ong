About Kiri Ong

Kiri Ong is an educator who writes historical adventures that teach children to handle money wisely, steady their feelings and respect people from other cultures.

The Trade Route Quest series is made of pick-your-path adventures for ages 8 to 12. Every choice is a decision about money, feelings or people. There are no dice and no luck: everything that happens comes from a choice the reader made, and when a path goes wrong, there was always a clue.

The first adventure follows a young apprentice on the Tang dynasty Silk Road in the year 745. Later books follow the same merchant family through history.

Some details in the books are true history and some are invented for the game. Each book has a note at the back that says which is which.
